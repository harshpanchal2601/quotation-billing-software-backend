Reporting uses one repeatable-read DB transaction. All period dates are explicit;
UTC timestamp windows include the complete through-date. Invoice/quotation/bill
business dates retain their existing source semantics. Receivables are CURRENT,
use the billing calendar in Asia/Kolkata and share the captured report clock.
Stock is CLOSING before the exclusive through-date boundary, capped at generation.
Current status/acceptance is not represented as historical status at period end.
Financial amounts stay strings and are segmented by currency without implicit FX.
Document sales include tax and are expressly not labeled recognized GL revenue.
Stock valuation retains six decimal places; unlike item quantities are not summed.

Unavailable sections contain no totals. Non-authorization failures propagate,
including receivables reconciliation errors; failures do not become false zeros.
CSV carries firm/schema/date/source/currency provenance and formula-safe text.
Saved filters confer no rights to the underlying data when rerun.

Manufacturing is one-level, whole-order production in one warehouse, without MRP,
partial completion, WIP, labor/overhead capitalization or a general ledger. BOM
ratios must fit current item unit precision exactly, without silently rounding a
recipe. Completion consumes available stock at existing moving-average ledger
cost, then receives output at the exact total component value. Cancellation
appends original-value reversals and can fail if output stock/value is unavailable
or reserved. Item units and currency changes are checked against frozen sources.

Request budgets use the existing express-rate-limit infrastructure: reporting
30/minute and manufacturing 60/minute per firm/actor, currently process-local.
No report files are stored by CSV export, and no legal-document/storage quota is
consumed by summary reads or metadata. New manufacturing-count quotas were not
invented. Document-capacity-zero fixtures exercise applicable read independence.
